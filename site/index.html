FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. The map, tracker and Discord are free. An optional supporter tier (no ads, cloud sync, route planner) comes later.

Can I submit someone else's clip?

No, only clips you recorded yourself. That's how we can credit everyone fairly and keep the channels online.

Do you sell money drops, mod menus or accounts?

No, never. They get accounts banned. Anyone offering them in our name is a scammer.

Is this official?

No. Full Clear is an independent fan community, not affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.
