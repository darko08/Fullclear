🎁 **HOW TO: find your Ultimate Edition items**

• **Pause → Store → Ultimate Edition.** Every item is listed with **when it unlocks** (by chapter).
• **Jason's motorbike:** available once you reach his house in Key Lento (after the roof repair); it's parked **right under the house**. Can't see it? Pin it from the Store screen and it shows on the map.
• **Car build (Ganado Retro):** apply it at **Coastal Auto Care in Key Lento** (Pro Builds).
• **Personalized weapon variants:** from the **locker at weapon shops**, for Jason in Chapter 1, for Lucia in Chapter 2.
• More unlocks (outfits, cars, Vintage Vice City Pack items) come in later chapters.
