**🛣️ Let the game drive for you (auto-drive)**
1. Set a destination on the map
2. Get your vehicle onto a road
3. **Hold D-pad down** for the cinematic camera
4. Press **Options** and choose auto-drive
Use L2/R2 to switch between 3 speeds, and press Circle to stop. It works with almost any motor vehicle. It's slower than driving yourself, but handy when you need a break.
