🦶 **Why does GTA VI have Bigfoot?** Rockstar answered.

Rockstar North's **Rob Nelson** told Kotaku the team loves keeping players unsure whether an encounter is real or staged, and that Florida's weirdness fits. That's why the **Skunk Ape** and UFO stories exist.
