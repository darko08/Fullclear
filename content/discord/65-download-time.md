**⏱️ How long does GTA 6 take to download? (our own maths)**
Rockstar says the file can be **up to 212 GB**, depending on your console. At a constant full speed, that works out to:
• **10 Mbps:** ~47 h
• **25 Mbps:** ~19 h
• **50 Mbps:** ~9.5 h
• **100 Mbps:** ~4 h 45 min
• **300 Mbps:** ~1.5 h
• **1 Gbps:** ~30 min
