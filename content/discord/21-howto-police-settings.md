🚨 **HOW TO: lose the cops + 2 settings worth changing**

**Police**
• Cops remember your **outfit**. Changing clothes is the quickest way to cool down. Tip: store outfits in your car's trunk at the safehouse. *(VGC, Kotaku)*
• At **1 star** you can sometimes talk your way out: no weapon out, hold LT/L2 on the cop, pick "defuse". Not every cop listens. *(Kotaku)*
• A **red icon** on a stolen car = tracker. Ditch it. *(Kotaku)*

**Settings**
• No performance mode on console: it's a **30 fps** target. On OLED TVs, try the **motion blur** slider (Settings → Display) if motion looks choppy. *(TechRadar, Push Square)*
• **Accessible Combat** and **Reduced Vehicle Damage**: Settings → Gameplay. *(Push Square)*
