**🚙 Wyman's Classic Car Collection (Special / Ultimate Edition)**
• **5 classic cars**, built from **15 parts** you find around Leonida and scan with **WAiNK**
• It opens in **Chapter 2**: after you talk to Wyman and finish the first Thrillbilly Mud Club race, he texts you
• Every car costs **$1,500** to build, and Wyman needs about a day between builds
• Build 4 of them and pay $1,000 more: you get the 5th car plus a jacket
• One part sits inside a shipping container, and you need the grinder to open it (you get the grinder midway through Chapter 3)
Full guide with all 15 part locations: **LunarGaming (CC BY)** <https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uEb1_rhWjmI>
