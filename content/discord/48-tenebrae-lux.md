**🎮 Hidden in GTA 6: a whole extra game, "Tenebrae Lux"**
On the in-game **JoyStation** console there's a 2–3 hour puzzle game credited entirely to a made-up "visionary" developer. Kotaku says it works as a joke about auteur game culture and as a genuinely good puzzle game.
Reportedly, collecting the notes unlocks a secret ending. We won't spoil it, so please keep it in spoiler tags here.
