**🤿 Kenton's Treasures beyond the shipwrecks**
Two more dive spots hold treasures you can sell to Kenton, even though there's no wreck at either:
• **Belpoint Strait**, just off Belpoint's southern tip on the Bobcat Bight side (a deep dive): 5 treasures, among them the Golden Fish and Lover's Grail
• **Bobcat Dive Site**, west of Bobcat Key: 6 treasures, among them the Astrolabe and Ivory Sundial
These don't count toward Underwater Discoveries, but they sell well. Bring scuba gear and a metal detector.
Source: Push Square
