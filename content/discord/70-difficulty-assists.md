🎮 **Is there an easy mode in GTA 6?**

No overall difficulty setting, but there are two assists that make it easier:
• **Accessible Combat**: enemies do less damage to you and go down faster
• **Reduced Vehicle Damage**: much harder to wreck your car in a crash
• Lock-on can be toggled there too

**Where:** Start → Settings → use the bumpers to go to the **Gameplay** tab.
Source: VGC (Nov 19, 2026)
