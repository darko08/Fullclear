📸 **Collectibles: phone scans + shipwrecks**

**WAiNK scans:** point your phone camera at landmarks, sculptures and odd "curiosities" to fill the collection. Easy first ones:
• **Wonder Whale** sculpture next to the highway in Key Lento
• **Fingerpost** signpost hidden in trees on the north side of the biggest Permit Keys island
• **Belpoint Lighthouse**, west of Capri
• **Princess Robot Bubblegum** statue by the Y Hotel entrance, Washington Beach
*(Push Square WAiNK guides)*

**Shipwrecks:** dive with scuba gear and a metal detector off Key Lento's southeast coast, dig up artefacts and sell them to Kenton. *(Push Square)*
