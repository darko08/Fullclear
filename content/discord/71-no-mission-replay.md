⚠️ **You can't replay missions in GTA 6 (yet)**

• There is currently **no mission replay**, and it's unknown if Rockstar will add it.
• The game keeps only **one autosave**, and you **can't manual-save during a mission**. If a mission matters to you, save at a safehouse before you start it.
• **Robberies can be repeated**: a robbed place is greyed out on the map until enough time passes.

Sources: VGC (Nov 18, 2026), Push Square (saves guide)
