**📦 Boxed copy with a missing or already-used code?**
Some players say their physical GTA 6 box came with a code that was missing or had already been redeemed. Rockstar hasn't commented.
If it happens to you:
• Keep the box and your receipt, and take a photo of the code card
• Go back to the **store you bought it from** first (exchange or refund)
• Then contact PlayStation or Xbox support with your receipt. Rockstar Support is down a lot right now
• **Never** buy "replacement codes" from strangers. That's a scam
Source: Dexerto (player reports)
