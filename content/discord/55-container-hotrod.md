**🔥 A hot rod hidden in a shipping container**
At a rail yard there's a locked shipping container that holds a unique hot rod, the **Caliente Picador**.
• Use the **bulldozer** parked in the yard to shove the other containers out of the way
• Open the locked one with the **grinder**. The car is inside and gets added to your Hot Rods list
LunarGaming's video shows the exact spot on the map. Found it yourself? Post the location with **/submit** and we'll pin it.
