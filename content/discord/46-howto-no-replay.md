**💾 There's no mission replay, so protect your progress**
Several outlets report that GTA 6 has **no replay menu for story missions**: during a mission you can only restart or go back to a checkpoint. Rockstar hasn't said this officially.
What to do:
• **Make a manual save before every mission**, and rotate between a few slots
• Autosave doesn't save everything (see our saving guide above)
• **Never switch off the console while it's saving.** One streamer lost 10+ hours this way this week
Sources: Insider Gaming · Beebom (no replay) · LevelUp (lost save story)
