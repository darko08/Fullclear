**🔐 Cold War mystery: two unique weapons in hidden bunkers**
1. Talk to the man at the **War Memorial in Vice City** to start the mystery
2. Head to the bunker on **Sharktooth Island**. The safe inside holds **Russo's 1922 pistol**
3. A second bunker is hidden in the woods in **Mariana County**, through a hatch in the ground. It has the **Cold War rifle**
The door and safe codes are in the full guide. Try to solve it yourself first, it's more fun.
