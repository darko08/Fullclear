💵 **HOW TO: keep your cash safe + find enterable buildings**

• **Before you have a bank account:** stash cash under the **floorboards in Jason's bedroom**. The **BuckMe** account unlocks in Chapter 1; after that, deposit at ATMs. Cash in your pocket is lost if you're wasted or busted. *(VGC)*
• **Which buildings can I go into?** The **dark-blue** ones on the map. The minimap also shows back doors. *(VGC, The Big Lead)*
• **Quick ones:** sleep to skip the night · call the mechanic if your car gets wrecked. *(VGC)*
