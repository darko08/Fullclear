**⚠️ "Rockstar apologised, fix comes Nov 25"? No official source**
A post doing the rounds on X claims Rockstar has apologised and promised a patch on Nov 25. We checked the Newswire, Rockstar Support and Rockstar's official channels and found nothing like it. X readers have also added a note disputing the post.
• The latest update is still **01.002.001**, and no patch notes have been published
• Rockstar hasn't announced any fix date
We'll post it here when there's an official patch. Until then, don't trust screenshots of "announcements".
