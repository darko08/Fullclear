**💰 Hidden cash: the Exotic Animals Front (Key Lento)**
On Key Lento there's a walled yard behind the **Three Sheets Liquor** store, just north of the Pay 'n' Spray. Inside, exotic birds are being smuggled under armed guard.
• Climb the fence, then take out the 5–6 guards or sneak past them
• Talk to the parrot in the cage. It drops a hint about hidden money
• Look in the **vent beside the generator** for about **$1.2k**
Most players stumble on it during an early errand in Key Lento. Confirmed by two outlets.
