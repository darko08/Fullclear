# 🃏 Collectibles: what we know so far
Two independent guides agree on **480 collectible cards in 18 sets** (PowerPyx, Push Square). Other counts floating around (flamingos, stunt jumps, underwater discoveries…) are **not confirmed yet**: guides disagree.
✅ Check the in-game **Map → Progress** menu and post what it says in #help-where-is-it.
