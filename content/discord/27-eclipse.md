🏎️ **The green "Fast & Furious" sports car is in GTA VI**

It sits in a **double garage in central Vice City** (approximate pin on our map). Breaking in sets off an alarm, but it stops on its own.
⚠️ **Don't grab it too early:** it won't stay yours unless you **register it with Moses** (you meet him around Chapter 2). Registering cost about **$6,500+** in DPJ's game.
