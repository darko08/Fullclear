# 🔄 Switching Jason ↔ Lucia & fast travel (from guides, confirm in your game)
• **Switch characters:** D-pad **Right** + **R1/RB**. Unlocks in **Chapter 2**. Greyed out = the other one is busy on a mission. *(Push Square)*
• **Fast travel:** call a **RydeMe** taxi; you can skip the ride for **$100**. Switching characters can be a cheaper way to "travel". *(Cultured Vultures, Kotaku)*
• Rumour check: the **standard box has a poster, not the map**. The official map is free from Rockstar, and it's the base of our tracker: https://fullclearmap.com/map/ *(Push Square, Rockstar)*
