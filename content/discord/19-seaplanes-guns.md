✈️ **Your first plane is 2.5 miles from home**

From Jason's place in Key Lento, drive southwest on Route 1A over the two long bridges to **Southwind**. Turn into the boatworks / Chicki's Tiki Bar lot next to Mile Zero Air Station: **seaplanes** sit in the hangar facing the water and at the dock. Nobody guards them. *(PC Gamer)*

🔫 Need ammo first? **Barrage Gun Supply** is right there in Southwind. *(Push Square)*
