**👕 Keep spare outfits in your car (the fastest way to lose the cops)**
• Every piece of clothing you buy or get from a mission goes to your **safehouse** wardrobe
• A hat or item picked up off someone isn't saved **until you go back to the safehouse**
• You can store **several outfits in the trunk** of Jason's and Lucia's cars, but only if you **save them to the car from the safehouse menu**. Buying clothes doesn't put them there.
• Police track what you're wearing, so changing outfit from the trunk is the **quickest way to drop your wanted level**
