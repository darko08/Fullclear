**⚖️ GTA 6 leak case: guilty plea, sentencing Monday**
The UK Crown Prosecution Service says **Arion Kurtaj (21)** pleaded guilty to a series of hacks, Rockstar Games among them, and to leaking **90 clips of GTA VI** in 2022. He will be sentenced at **Woolwich Crown Court on Monday, Nov 23**. We'll post the outcome once it's official.
