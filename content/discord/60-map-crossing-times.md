**🗺️ How big is Leonida? Times to cross it from south to north**
Kotaku timed the trip from the southern tip to the far north of the map:
• **On foot (walking):** 3 h 21 min 20 s
• **Running/sprinting:** 1 h 41 min. Jason and Lucia have a stamina bar, so they can't sprint forever like in GTA V
• **Mid-range sedan on the highways:** 10 min 53 s
For comparison, running across GTA V's map takes about 30 min.
