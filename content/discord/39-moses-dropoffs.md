• **Western Nightblade:** biker clubhouse on Birch St, Ambrosia. In over the roof, open the gate first.
• **Declasse Vamos:** locked garage on Rockledge Rd east of Grayson. The code is inside the main house.
• **BF Bandito:** trail chase in north-east Buckskin Swamp. Tag it with WAiNK, then take it.
