🦶 **Bigfoot is real (in Leonida)**

The **Skunk Ape** can be found near **Key Lento**: across the bay from Jason's first house, at the end of a dead-end road on a mangrove peninsula. Easiest by boat from his dock. Use **Focus Mode** to follow the noises into the mangroves, then check the effigy on a tree. *(PC Gamer, GamesRadar)*
