# 🛠️ GTA VI won't start on PS5? Sony's official checklist
1. **Is it out in your region yet?** It unlocks at local midnight.
2. **Right account?** Sign in with the PSN account that bought it.
3. **Download failed / padlock?** Settings → Users and Accounts → Other → **Restore Licenses**.
4. **Missing Ultimate Edition bonuses?** They unlock through the story, new ones every chapter.
5. **PS5 system update 14.23** is required to play.
*Source: PlayStation Support (playstation.com/support/games/gta-vi-game)*
