👁️ **GTA 6 Focus mode: the colours explained**

Click **both sticks** to turn on Focus mode. It highlights what matters around you:
• 🔴 **Red**: enemies
• 🟡 **Yellow**: things you can use
• 🟢 **Green**: cash
• **Doors:** white = you can open it, red = alarmed (expect trouble)

Also handy for tracking noises and clues on side activities.
