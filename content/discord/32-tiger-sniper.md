🐅 **Legendary sniper guarded by a tiger (Pelican Valley)**

East of **Pelican Valley**, near a small airstrip, a tiger attacks the people at a run-down camp. Let the fight end, then loot a few thousand in cash and a **unique sniper rifle**. Watch for men arriving by car, and leave fast: police may show up. Store the sniper in your **own** car's trunk so you don't lose it.
