🧘 **Meditation spots (they count toward a trophy)**

Sit on the mat and do the breathing minigame:
• **Water Wheel:** south of MacAlister Gold Mine, Moreau
• **Buckskin Swamp:** end of the pier next to the lookout tower
• **Smooth Sand:** stone pattern on the northern sandbar of Belpoint
