⚙️ **HOW TO: 6 settings worth changing (DPJ's picks)**

• **No graphics modes** on console: it's a 30 fps target, so don't hunt for a performance mode.
• **Movement speed:** let the stick control walk/jog so you don't need to hold sprint.
• **Stand & Sprint: OFF**: otherwise an accidental sprint pops you out of cover.
• **View button → Map** for one-press map access.
• **Tap assist ×2** if button-mash prompts are hard; **fishing on "hold"**; **auto-equip helmet ON**.
• Making videos? Audio has an option to **turn off licensed music**.
