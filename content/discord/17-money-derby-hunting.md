💸 **Two legit early money methods**

**1. Demolition derby at Ambrosia Raceway Park** (north of Ambrosia). Repeatable. The figure-eight pays roughly double the oval, and you can rent a car at the track. *(PC Gamer)*
**2. Hunting for Bret** (from Chapter 2). His cabin is off Sassafrass Rd in Mount Kalaga National Park. Sell animals from your trunk and take his bounty lists, including legendary animals. *(PC Gamer)*

Source: <https://www.pcgamer.com/games/grand-theft-auto/gta-6-how-to-make-money/>
