📸 **GTA 6 Photo Studio: the settings most players skip**

Players are calling GTA 6 a "cinematographer simulator" because of photo mode. What it actually lets you do:
• **Lighting layers**: add light sources to the scene, move them, resize them, and set **intensity** and **beam angle**
• **Depth of field**, **exposure**, **film grain** and **aspect ratio**
• **17 filters** plus a **vignette** that pulls the eye to your subject

Tip: one soft light from the side plus shallow depth of field beats any filter.
