🚨 **PSA: there is no GTA VI on PC**

GTA VI is out on **PS5 and Xbox Series X|S** only. Rockstar hasn't announced a PC version. "GTA 6 PC download / installer" sites are fake: Malwarebytes found ones that install an **infostealer** (grabs saved passwords and logged-in sessions).
Don't download, don't share. Ran one? Change your passwords from a clean device.
Source: <https://www.malwarebytes.com/blog/news/2026/08/fake-gta-6-extended-look-and-demo-sites-deliver-an-infostealer>
