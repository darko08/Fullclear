**🚗 How to keep the cars you steal (the whole system)**
• **Unregistered stolen cars don't stay yours.** You lose them if you die, get busted, leave them or restart
• **Scan first:** point WAiNK at a car to see access, security, sell value, registration cost and **tracker** (none / standard / advanced)
• **Advanced trackers** reportedly alert the police when you slow down, so keep moving
• **Chapter 2:** Moses (Watkins Auto Parts, Port Gellhorn) lets you register: steal a car **with no tracker**, respray it, register. Don't steal it right next to his garage
• ⚠️ **Check the registration price.** It can cost far more than the car is worth
• You need a **free garage or parking space**, otherwise registration is refused
• Impounded? Pay at the lot or drive out (expect stars). Lost a registered car? Order it through the **Scooter Bros** app (costs a fee)
