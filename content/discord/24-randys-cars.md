🚗 **Randy's wish list: 4 cars you can grab**

• **Pegassi Infernus:** level 7 of the Crosstown Police Station car park (NW 9th St). Slip in after a police car leaves.
• **Pegassi Vitello:** villa garage on Flamingo Pkwy, Waning Sands.
• **Bravado Banshee:** outside the Sweetbay Inn, Capri, around **3 AM** in-game.
• **Bravado Gauntlet Hellfire:** back of the M7 junkyard east of Boone Speedway, Hamlet.
