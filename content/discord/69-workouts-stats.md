💪 **GTA 6 fitness: why your stats can drop**

• **Strength** = health and how much damage you can take. **Cardio** = stamina and sprinting. **Focus** = how long you can slow time in combat.
• Open the **Fitness app** on your phone to see your **Workout Windows**. Do a Strength or Cardio workout inside the window, or that stat **goes down**.
• **Focus never goes down**. It can only increase.
• A protein bar or shake right after a workout shortens the wait until the next window.
• Bonus: petting dogs and cats buffs Focus, and food and drink boost health, stamina and Focus.

You don't *have* to work out, but it makes fights easier.
