**GTA 6: $3.79 billion on day one (official)**

Take-Two just confirmed it:
• Day-one retail sales: **$3.79 billion** (company estimate, through the end of Nov 19 worldwide). That makes it the biggest entertainment launch in history, across any medium.
• **32.5 million** copies sold *before* launch, a record across all platforms.

Earlier today the "~25M copies" number came from Sensor Tower, an outside estimate. These are Take-Two's own figures.
Source: Take-Two press release, Nov 20 (take2games.com/ir)
