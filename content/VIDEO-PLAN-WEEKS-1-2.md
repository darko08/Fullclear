# Full Clear — video plan, launch weeks 1–2

**Goal of every video in this phase: get viewers into the Discord.** Views are nice; Discord joins are the KPI.
Each platform gets its own invite link so we can see which one works (Server Settings → Invites).

---

## 0. The hosts: the Full Clear crew

Four hosts: **Scout** (camera), **Nia** (map tablet), **Mei** (binoculars), **Dev** (notebook).
Each video picks one at random (never the same twice in a row). Fixed voices and script rules are in
[CREW.md](CREW.md). In the scripts below, "Scout" means "the host of that video": swap in the name.

* **Rules:** never claim to be human or official. Tick the platform's **AI-generated content** label
  on every video where the crew appears.

## 4. Format scripts

**F1 · Secret location** (20–25s)
> HOOK caption: *"99% of players drive right past this"*
> SCOUT: "Scout here. You missed something."
> [Gameplay: drive to [AREA], reveal [FIND]]
> SCOUT VO: "Head to [AREA]. Look for [LANDMARK]. Behind it: [FIND]. It gets you [REWARD]."
> CAPTION over map: pin drops on [AREA]
> END CARD: "Found one we haven't? Link in bio."

**F2 · Don't-miss tip** (20–30s)
> HOOK: *"Do this BEFORE [MISSION/POINT] or it's gone"* (only use if verified true; never fake urgency)
> SCOUT (shock eyes): "Seriously. Do this first."
> [Gameplay of the tip]
> SCOUT VO: "[TIP in one sentence]. Miss it and [CONSEQUENCE]."
> END: "More tips from the community, link in bio."

**F3 · Money method** (30–40s)
> HOOK: *"I tested [N] ways to make money. #1 pays $[X]/hour"*
> SCOUT ($ $ eyes): "Ranked. Timed. No mod menus, no glitches that get you banned."
> [Gameplay montage, on-screen: #3 $[X]/hr → #2 $[Y]/hr → #1 $[Z]/hr]
> SCOUT VO: "Number one: [METHOD]. Full breakdown on the map site."
> END: "Got a faster one? Prove it in our Discord."

**F4 · Collect-them-all** (30–45s)
> HOOK: *"All [N] [COLLECTIBLE] in [TIME]"*
> [Sped-up route with map overlay ticking 1/[N] … [N]/[N]]
> SCOUT VO: "Every one is on the free tracker. Tick them off as you go."
> END: "Map link in bio."

**F5 · Easter egg** (15–25s)
> HOOK: *"Rockstar hid THIS in [AREA]"*
> [Clip of the easter egg]
> SCOUT (heart eyes): "[What it references] … they really did that."
> END: "Which one did you find? Comment or drop it in the Discord."

**F6 · Detail / "did you know"** (10–20s)
> HOOK: *"The game remembers if you [ACTION]…"*
> [Clip showing the detail]
> SCOUT: "[One-line payoff]. The details in this game are unreal."

**F7 · Community find** (15–20s): **the most important format for growing Discord**
> HOOK: *"[@member] found this on day [N]"*
> LOWER THIRD the whole time: *Found by @[member] · Full Clear Discord*
> [Member's clip]
> SCOUT VO: "Shout-out to [@member]: [FIND] at [AREA]. That's +25 points and a spot in today's video."
> END (Scout): "You could be next. Link in bio."
> → Then post the video in #featured-in-videos and tag them in Discord.

**F8 · Top Scouts of the week** (20–30s)
> HOOK: *"These 3 players found the most secrets this week"*
> [Top 3 from `/leaderboard`, each with their best clip, 5s each]
> SCOUT: "Want your name here next Sunday? Link in bio."

## 6. What to measure (daily, 5 minutes)

| Metric | Where | Target by day 14 |
|---|---|---|
| Discord joins per platform | Server Settings → Invites | 1,000+ members total |
| Submissions per day | `#clip-review` | 20+ |
| Best format | Platform analytics: views + profile visits | Make more of the top 2 formats |
| Follow-through | Discord joins ÷ profile visits | > 5% |

If a format gets views but no Discord joins, change its end card before making more of it.
