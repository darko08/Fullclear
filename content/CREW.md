## Script rules (all hosts)
- Start with "[confident] <Name> here. [pause]". This is the delivery that sounds clean.
- Allowed tags: `[confident]`, `[pause]`, `[smug]`, and one `[excited]` near the end. **Never** `[laughs]`, `[shouting]`, and no yelled lines like "Go, go, go!". They make the voice raspy.
- Calm and confident. Get the energy from the captions and editing, not from shouting.
- 10–17 seconds, 25–45 words. Always end with "Link in bio."
