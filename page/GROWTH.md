# Growth plan: 1,000 followers on every network, then 5,000

Owner's mandate (2026-11-18): the boss decides alone; goal = 1,000 followers on Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and X, then 5,000.
Limits that still apply: platform rules, Rockstar ToS (no Rockstar content in AI tools), no leaks, **no bought followers, no bots,
no follow/unfollow, no fake engagement, no spam comments**, and no spending money without the owner's explicit yes.

## Why people will follow us
1. **Fastest verified GTA 6 news** (official sources only) → "follow for daily GTA 6 news".
2. **Useful**: HOW TO tips, unlock times, help, the free map + 100% tracker.
3. **Series** that make people come back: Postcards 1/6, "How long does it take…", Day X in Leonida, Question of the day.
4. **Community**: finds from our Discord with the finder's name on screen → they share it → their friends follow.

## Engines
- **Newsroom** (every 4 h): scouts find → boss verifies → posts. Every real news item also becomes a **News short** (`tools/news_short.py`, ~1 credit).
- **Crew videos** (Runway, ~130 credits each): 1/day max while credits last; best formats get repeated.
- **Community finds**: every approved Discord find with a clip → credited short.
- **Cross-promo**: website and Discord point to all four profiles; every video ends with @fullclearmap.

## Tactics
- Hook in the first second; captions burned in; 7–30 s.
- Every caption ends with a question (comments push reach).
- "Part X/6" and "follow for part 2" series.
- Instagram **Trial Reels** for new formats (shown to non-followers first).
- Post when the audience is awake: US evening (00:00–04:00 CET) and EU evening (18:00–22:00 CET).
- Read Postiz analytics every evening → double down on the top format, drop the bottom one (learning.md).
