# Clickbait that serves gamers (owner's focus, 2026-11-20)

**Rule:** the title/thumbnail makes a strong promise, and the video keeps it within the first 5 seconds, then teaches something.
Honest clickbait = curiosity + a real, verified answer. Never fake ("FREE GTA 6 download", "play early", fake leaks, fake thumbnails).
Before publishing ask: (1) Does the video answer the title? (2) Does the viewer learn something they can use in the game? (3) Could any other page post this exact thing? If yes → change the angle (our map, our crew, our own measurements).

## Our formulas (adapt, never copy wording)
- **"NEVER do X in GTA 6 (do this instead)"** → a mechanic + fix (saves, cops, cash).
- **"GTA 6 [system] is SMARTER than you think 😳"** → cops remember outfits, NPC memory, trackers.
- **"FREE [vehicle/weapon] in your first hour"** → our map pin + clip + where exactly.
- **"I measured [thing] in GTA 6 so you don't have to"** → our own data (drive times, map size, prices). Only we have it.
- **"X things you NEED to change/do first (no spoilers)"** → settings, starter guides (long + cut into Shorts).
- **"[Place] vs real [place]"** → Leonida vs real Florida (official shots + real photos we're allowed to use).
- **"What happens if you… [extreme]"** → Leonida Daily stories from CC BY clips.
- **"Myth or fact: [viral claim]"** → checked against facts.md; the answer is the payoff.
- **"Ranked: [category] from worst to best"** → cars, safehouses, side activities (with sources).
- **"POV:" skits** with our crew (our animation, voice): relatable gamer moments.

## Thumbnails
3–4 words max, one face (our host) + one object (car/heli/map pin), arrow/circle on the real thing, high contrast, pink/teal brand.
Never text on Rockstar images (they stay unmodified): use CC BY gameplay frames (adaptation allowed, credited) or our own art.

## Output mix (YouTube, max 4 uploads/day via Postiz)
- 1 long video/day at most (8–12 min when material allows: 8+ min unlocks mid-roll ads once monetized) — best day slot.
- 2–3 Shorts/day: cut from long videos (`podcast.py --clip`) + Leonida Daily + news.
- TikTok/IG: all Shorts, 3–5/day, spread.

## Every Short also teaches
End line: "Full guide on our channel / pinned on our free map" → drives long-video watch time + map visits.
