## 4. Credit and permission rules
1. Public posts only. Skip anything protected, deleted or taken down.
2. Credit on its own line: `🎥 @creator`. Official material: `🎥 @RockstarGames`. Photos: `📸 @creator`.
3. Credit is not permission. Ask first:
   > Hey! Love this clip. We run Full Clear (@fullclearmap), a GTA 6 community. OK if we share it with full credit and a tag? Happy to shout you out in our Discord too.
4. Transform every clip: trim, on-screen hook, captions, our tip/commentary. Never a plain re-upload.
5. Asked to take something down → remove it the same day, no argument, log it in `takedowns.md`, don't use that creator again without permission.
6. Never: leaks, datamined files, leaked builds/maps/tracklists, pirated copies, "play early" tricks, exploits.
7. Never name leak outlets or rumour sources.
8. Rockstar footage is theirs; fan pages run on their tolerance. Short official clips only with our commentary, preferably on TikTok/IG/X; **YouTube Content ID often claims official footage**, so avoid it there.

## 5. Copy rules
- X: no hashtags (#GTA6 at most once), short headline, bullets, credit last. **Links get stripped by Postiz on X**, so say "link in bio".
- Instagram: hook + 1–2 sentences + credit + 3–5 hashtags. TikTok/Shorts: short caption + a few hashtags.
- Only verified facts. Never anything that reads as official Rockstar communication. Don't repeat a caption within a week.
- AI label ON for every post where the crew appears.

## 6. Specs
9:16 · 1080×1920 · H.264 + AAC · 7–30 s for clips. X accepts up to 140 s.

## 7. Launch-day playbook (Nov 19)
- Unlocks roll out region by region (earliest time zones first). Wording until an official page confirms the exact time: "unlocking region by region".
- First legit gameplay → clip within 15–30 min → X first, then Reels/TikTok/Shorts.
- Post every 1–2 hours for 48 h: driving, map, graphics, NPCs, funny moments, hidden details. **No story spoilers in hooks**; label "SPOILER" in captions.
- Every repeated question in comments → a HOW TO post the same day.

## 8. Engagement (2–3×/day, ~15 min)
Reply in the first hour on our posts; useful replies under official posts (a verified fact or quick tip); thank credited creators; save good questions to `idea-bank.md`. Never argue, never leak drama, never "follow me" spam.

## 9. Learning loop
Log every post in `log.md` + `scoreboard.csv` (12 h / 24 h / 72 h reads from Postiz analytics). One test per day, one variable. Weekly review → `learning.md`.

## 10. Check-ins with the owner
Daily short report (what posted, top post with numbers, one lesson, tomorrow's test). Approvals in one message with exact caption + media so the owner can reply "yes". Flag takedowns, wrong posts and failed uploads immediately.

## 10. Craft tactics (from owner's inspiration "Faceless GTA 6 Clipping Page", page 1–2, taken 2026-11-18)
- **Payoff before second 8.** Clips 7–20 s; first frame = one line of hook text on screen.
- **Loop endings:** last frame leads back into the first (rewatches).
- **Same clip, different first line per platform** → we learn twice as fast; log which line won in learning.md.
- **Answer the first 10 comments within the hour** of every post (owner's Discord excluded; never spam).
- **Hook bank** (adapt, never copy): "Nobody noticed this in [PLACE]", "I tested [MYTH] so you don't have to", "[N] seconds of pure chaos", "Tell me this isn't real life", "The fastest way to [GOAL]". Two hooks tested on the same clip.
- **Read the numbers:** watch % → reuse hook · rewatches → same ending · saves → more tips · shares → make a series · follows → pin it. Compare only with our own average.
- **Week 2+ series:** "Day N in Leonida", "Every car ranked", "Secrets part N".
