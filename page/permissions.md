# Clip permissions: only clips from these sources may be used

Every entry needs **proof** (link or screenshot path) and the **conditions** (credit format, no monetisation, etc.).
No proof → not allowed. If a creator withdraws permission → remove their clips the same day and log it in `takedowns.md`.

| Source | Proof | Conditions |
|---|---|---|
| Our Discord `/submit` | Clip licence in #rules + `consent: true` on every submission | Credit the `credit` name on screen |
| Official Rockstar media (rockstargames.com/VI/media) | Page says "Download and share official videos, screenshots, and more." | Unmodified (trim only), "© Rockstar Games", never into AI tools |
| YouTube videos under Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY) | Licence shown under the video | Credit creator + link in description |
